# Problem Statement

Date: 2026-05-18

## Context

Educational institutions and training organizations need a reliable way to run online technical assessments and interviews while maintaining exam integrity, reducing manual administration overhead, and scaling across multiple organizations and user roles.

## Core Problem

Current assessment workflows are fragmented:

- Test creation and assignment are manual and time-consuming.
- Monitoring of candidate behavior during tests is inconsistent.
- Integrity events are hard to capture and audit in a structured way.
- Interview coordination and signaling between participants requires separate tools.
- Multi-tenant organization administration (admin, subadmin, student, superadmin) lacks centralized controls.

## Business Impact of the Problem

- Higher risk of malpractice and disputed outcomes.
- Delayed candidate evaluation and hiring/academic decisions.
- Increased operational cost for invigilation and admin support.
- Limited visibility into attempt logs, submissions, and organization-level performance.

## Target Outcome

Deliver a unified assessment and interview platform that:

- Supports organization-based onboarding and role-based access.
- Enables test authoring, assignment, execution, and submission workflows end to end.
- Captures proctoring and integrity signals with audit trails.
- Supports interview room joining and signaling with controlled access.
- Provides superadmin controls for top-level account governance.

## Success Definition

Etester is successful when organizations can run exams and interviews using one system with:

- Lower administrative effort,
- Improved trust in assessment outcomes,
- Faster decision-making from structured result and integrity data.
